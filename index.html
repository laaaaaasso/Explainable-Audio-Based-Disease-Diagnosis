AUDIO DEMONSTRATIONS

Audio-based Disease Diagnosis

Explainable diagnosis through evidence-grounded reasoning

Speech, voice, respiratory sounds, and heart sounds carry clinically meaningful cues about health. Our framework grounds audio in human-readable, fine-grained semantic rubrics and adaptively combines this evidence with acoustic representations through cross-modal fusion, making disease predictions more interpretable.

The examples below showcase six recordings from VOC-ALS, RSD, and ADReSSo, covering neuromuscular, respiratory, and neurodegenerative conditions alongside healthy controls. Listen to each recording and explore its associated semantic rubric descriptions.

06 tasks
THE APPROACH

From sound to interpretable evidence

01

Define semantic rubrics

Build disease-specific descriptions using medical knowledge, language-model-assisted refinement, and expert review.

02

Ground evidence in audio

Align acoustic representations with semantic rubrics to identify fine-grained, human-readable evidence.

03

Fuse complementary cues

Integrate rubric evidence with acoustic features to support explainable disease classification.
